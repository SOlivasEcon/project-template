\section{Introduction}

The demo tasks simulate a panel of firms whose output follows a Cobb-Douglas production function
\citep{CobbDouglas1928},
\begin{equation}
  \log Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \beta_K \log K_{it} + \beta_L \log L_{it} + \varepsilon_{it},
\end{equation}
with $\beta_K = 0.35$ and $\beta_L = 0.6$. Table~\ref{tab:production} reports the estimates and
Figure~\ref{fig:output_labor} plots log output against log labor.
